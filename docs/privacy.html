🔥 FoodTrail

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

FoodTrail ("we", "our", or "the app") is built to help you rate individual dishes and share food trails with others. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — your email address, display name, and handle when you create an account.
  • Ratings and content — dish scores, "would go back" answers, written notes, and trails you create or follow.
  • Photos — photos of dishes you take and submit with a rating. These are stored on our servers and shown to other users.
  • Location (in-session only) — your approximate GPS location is used to suggest nearby restaurants when you add a dish. It is not stored in our database and is not linked to your account.

2. How we use your information

We use the information solely to operate FoodTrail:

  • Authenticate you and maintain your account.
  • Display your ratings, trails, and profile to other users.
  • Suggest nearby restaurants based on your current location.
  • Calculate dish scores using our scoring model.
  • Moderate content to keep the app safe (automated text and photo checks).

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not share it with third parties except as described below.

3. Third-party services

  • Supabase — our backend provider (database, file storage, authentication). Your data is stored on Supabase's infrastructure. Supabase Privacy Policy →
  • Apple MapKit — used to search for and display restaurants. Location queries are sent to Apple's servers under Apple's privacy policy.
  • OpenAI — photos and text you submit are briefly sent to OpenAI's API for content moderation (checking for inappropriate content). Images are not stored by OpenAI beyond the API call. OpenAI Privacy Policy →

4. Data retention

Your account data is kept for as long as your account exists. Ratings are append-only and form a public record of the dish's score history. If you delete your account, your personal information (name, email, handle) is removed; your ratings may remain in anonymised aggregate form to preserve score history.

5. Your rights

  • Access & export — contact us and we'll provide a copy of your data.
  • Correction — you can update your display name and handle in the app at any time (Settings → Edit Profile).
  • Deletion — you can delete your account from within the app (Profile → Delete Account). This is immediate and permanent.

6. Children

FoodTrail is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
